The process / How I work

Creative instinct.
A considered plan.

I start with the customer and the commercial goal, then connect research, creative decisions, and coordinated delivery.

✳
01Understand the customer+

Study customer behaviour, preferences, and the market. Prepare a clear view of what people respond to and where the brand has a useful opportunity.

02Give the work a direction+

Turn the findings into a practical brief: the positioning, product story, packaging direction, or campaign idea. Give each asset a purpose for the customer and the business.

03Create & coordinate+

Work with clients, designers, and sales teams to organise materials, schedules, and approvals. Keep the message consistent across packaging, social, email, presentations, and events.

04Measure & refine+

Review customer response and campaign performance, prepare reports, and turn the findings into the next action — from a content change to a new market or brand opportunity.

Case study 01 / Blairsom

From product to
personal ritual.

Australian personal care · Positioning, packaging, customer insights & campaigns

The brief

Build awareness and engagement for a natural personal care range while supporting e-commerce and repeat purchases.

My contribution

  • Contributed to brand positioning and packaging development, connecting customer preferences with product presentation.
  • Researched customers and market opportunities, prepared reports, and translated findings into marketing actions.
  • Produced campaigns around the brand’s product and ingredient stories.
  • Used UGC-led content across Instagram and Facebook to make the brand more relatable.
  • Ran targeted Meta and Google Ads for hair care, body wash, and gift products.
  • Created subscriber newsletters and EDMs using Mailchimp and Smartpush.
  • Managed Shopify content and coordinated with design teams on brand materials.

The thinking behind the work

Customer preferences give the product story a direction. Packaging and brand assets establish how the range is presented; content and email then carry that same story into everyday product use and repeat purchase.

Reported outcomes

30% higher customer engagement through UGC-led campaigns and 15% higher repeat purchase rates through retention-focused content and email marketing.

Making every
click count.

AU KingCare & Promagic · Customer insights, packaging, paid media & reporting

The brief

Support awareness and sales for Australian dairy and nutrition brands by connecting website content, advertising, and performance reporting.

My contribution

  • Used customer and market research to support positioning, campaigns, and commercial opportunities.
  • Contributed to B2B marketing and sales support alongside consumer-facing activity.
  • Ran and optimised campaigns across Meta, Amazon, and Google Ads.
  • Created SEO-focused content for the website, social channels, and email newsletters.
  • Researched keywords and improved on-page content using SEMrush and Ahrefs.
  • Tracked performance in GA4 and Search Console, with Excel dashboards for conversions and ROI.

Reported outcome

Reduced Amazon advertising cost of sales from 42% to under 13% through targeted campaign optimisation. ACOS measures ad spend as a share of attributed advertising sales; a lower percentage indicates less ad spend per dollar of attributed sales.

Performance reporting helps connect creative decisions to the customer journey, with lessons from paid campaigns informing content priorities and future campaign planning.
